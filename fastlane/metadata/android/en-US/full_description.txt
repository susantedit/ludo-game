GAME MODES
- Classic Ludo: The traditional 4-token strategy game for 2 to 4 players. Roll sixes to enter the track, capture opponent tokens, and race safely to the center home triangle.
- Snake & Ladder: The timeless 100-tile race with ladders that boost you forward and snakes that slide you back. Requires an exact roll to finish.
- Ludora Remix: A hybrid board mode combining Ludo navigation with snake and ladder hazard tiles, plus 5 tactical power cards (Shield, Speed Boost, Reroll, Swap, and Bomb).
