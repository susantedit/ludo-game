PLAYER-FIRST MANIFESTO

Fair Play & Monetization Guarantee

We respect your time, battery, and focus. Here is our binding pledge to every Ludora player.

1. Zero Mid-Game Advertisements

We guarantee that no banner, interstitial video, or popup ad will ever appear during an active gameplay turn. Your decisions, rolls, and board concentration are never broken by forced commercials.

2. Complete Offline Ownership

Ludora does not force internet connectivity or account creation. All game modes (Single Player vs AI and Pass-and-Play Local Multiplayer) function 100% offline. When offline, all advertising SDKs are suppressed to prevent blank layout containers and battery drain.

3. Transparent, Permanent In-App Purchases

We offer a single, optional "Remove Ads" pass that permanently disables menu banners and post-match interstitials across all your linked Google Play devices. Rewarded video bonuses for cosmetic coins remain completely voluntary.

4. Cryptographic Randomness (Zero Weighted Dice)

Every dice roll is generated using cryptographically secure pseudorandom number generators (CWE-330 compliant). We never weight rolls to artificially manipulate match outcomes or drive frustration.

2. Information Processed Locally on Device

The vast majority of gameplay data never leaves your hardware:

  • Local Game Database: Match logs, player statistics, quest progress, AI difficulty settings, and custom rule configurations are written to a sandboxed SQLite database using Android Jetpack Room with SQLite encryption.

When participating in online multiplayer matches:

AUTOMATED SECURITY AUDIT

Security & Vulnerability Protections

Our codebase underwent an automated static analysis audit to protect users from common mobile software vulnerabilities.

Common Weakness Enumeration (CWE) Mitigations

  • CWE-89 (SQL Injection Protection): All database interactions utilize parameterized Room DAO queries. Zero dynamic raw SQL concatenation exists across the codebase.
  • CWE-926 (Exported Android Components): All activities, receivers, and services in AndroidManifest.xml enforce android:exported="false" with the exception of the standard launch intent filter.
  • CWE-330 (Cryptographic Randomness): Dice tumbling and room token generation utilize java.security.SecureRandom to prevent predictability attacks.
  • CWE-312 (Cleartext Data Exposure): In-memory and local SQLite database files are sandboxed within private application storage volumes.
  • CWE-400 (Resource Exhaustion): WebSocket message payload sizes are capped at 4KB with strict per-second turn throttling.
